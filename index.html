How to teach it
  1. Press Start camera and allow access.
  2. Pick a sign (start with A, B and C), show it to the camera, then press Record.
  3. After a 3-second countdown, hold the sign and slowly tilt or move your hand while it records.
  4. Repeat for each sign. Once two or more signs are taught, choose “My taught signs” in the Recognized gesture panel.
The CSV matches the Python desktop app, so you can import data recorded there (and the other way round).
